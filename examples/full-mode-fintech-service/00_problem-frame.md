# Problem Frame: LedgerCore High-Performance Ledger

## Executive Summary
LedgerCore aims to replace the legacy monolith payment processor with a dedicated, high-performance double-entry ledger service. The primary driver is the inability of the current SQL-based row-locking mechanism to scale beyond 500 TPS, causing business losses during peak events.

## Key Constraints
The project is strictly bound by **PCI-DSS v4.0** requirements and must achieve **strong consistency** at scale, which eliminates eventual consistency models for balance calculation.
